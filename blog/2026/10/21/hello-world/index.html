Hello, world

October 21, 2026

Hello, world! I guess!?

The spark

I wrote my first Hello World in 10th grade, in Pascal. It was easy, but somehow it sparked something in me.

I was a lazy kid (I still am) and bad at almost everything in school. But after that Pascal program compiled, I got a 10 for the first time in my life.

I kept that spark bright and warm, and it carried me all the way to Japan as a programmer.

And then it died

Maybe it was the ever-so-busy life of a real-life Night City. Maybe the Land of the Rising Sun killed it. I’m not sure.

I hardly built anything after work anymore. Too tired, too drained, too busy to keep the spark alive.

Then, BOOM! AI!

I thought, “This is it.” This is where I go back home, a defeated, naive Homo sapiens.

A different kind of spark

Then one day, a friend from university showed me a game he had made with AI. It was nothing crazy. Pretty ordinary, as a matter of fact. But it worked!

So I tried it myself, and the spark came back.

It’s a different kind of spark, though. It isn’t the feeling of watching a seed sprout for the first time. It’s more like getting my first computer all over again: a tool that helps me turn ideas into something real, faster. It handles the mundane, boring parts while I focus on the difficult, interesting, hands-on ones.

So, here we are

I’m still not sure whether AI is good or bad, or whether it’s going to be the developer killer. Honestly, I don’t care anymore.

I’ve always wanted my own resume website and a blog, but I was always too busy.

Now, with AI, I can do it. This site is the proof.
